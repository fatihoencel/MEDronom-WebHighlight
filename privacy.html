MEDronom - WebHighlight Privacy Policy

MEDronom - WebHighlight lets you highlight and underline text on web pages and save the parts you choose as PDF files. The developer does not operate any server and receives no data from you.

1. Data the Extension handles

A. Highlights (stored locally)

The selected text, its color and style, a short piece of surrounding text and the page address are stored in chrome.storage.local on your device so your highlights reappear when you reopen a page. You can delete them from the extension popup; uninstalling removes all local data.

B. Optional Google Drive saving (off unless you set it up)

If you enter your own Google Apps Script web app address and turn on Drive saving, each highlight is turned into a PDF in your browser (highlighted text, a few lines before it and the sentence after it, page title and address) and sent to your own Apps Script address, which saves it into your Google Drive folder. Data travels only between your browser and your own Google account over HTTPS. Nothing is sent to the developer or any third party. Without this setup, no data leaves your browser.

3. What we do not do

We do not sell, rent or share user data, and do not use it for advertising or unrelated purposes. We collect no name, email, IP address, browsing history or passwords. There is no analytics, tracking or advertising code, and no remote code is loaded: the PDF library is bundled inside the Extension.
