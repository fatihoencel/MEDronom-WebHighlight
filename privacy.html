WebMark & PDF Studio Privacy Policy

WebMark & PDF Studio is designed with a privacy-first, 100% client-side architecture. We do not operate remote tracking servers, analytics engines, or databases. All data remains strictly in your own browser and your personal Google Drive account.

1. Data Collection & Processing

A. Local Highlights & Annotations

The text you highlight, colors, and styles are stored locally inside your browser via chrome.storage.local. No personal data is sent to us.

B. Google Drive Integration (OAuth 2.0)

When you enable Drive synchronization, the extension uses Google OAuth 2.0 (drive.file scope) to directly upload PDF files to your Google Drive under Drive'ım / Uygulama verileri / MEDronom Web Highlighter. The extension can only access files it creates, and cannot access any other files in your Google Drive.

2. Permissions Justification

PermissionJustification

activeTab / <all_urls>To highlight text and capture PDFs on the page you select.

3. Zero Third-Party Sharing

We do not sell, rent, monetize, or transmit user data. No tracking pixels, ads, or analytics scripts are included.
